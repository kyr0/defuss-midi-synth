Dear N64 Fans:

    I'm taking a little break from Zelda 64 MIDIs.
I'd like to introduce my first Mario Party MIDI:
DK's "Jungle Adventure". I am not too pleased with
this MIDI mainly because the drums cannot be as
well-made as the original song. Why?  MIDI is
limited, and only has basic drums, and none of that
techno stuff.  But, I did what I could with what I
had.  I strongly recommend wave synthesis with this.
FM Synth users will not be able to hear the "monkey"
sound (possibly a cuica, like I used for the sound).
In addition, this is the second version uploaded.
I have fixed a small problem with the drums that
shows on some sound fonts.  I honestly dislike making
new versions of MIDIs, so my apologies to anyone who
has already downloaded my first version.  This will
*hopefully* be the final on this MIDI.  Oh, and a
slight change on the information which is basically
to help people know which Boards belong to which
characters.  Also changed name from "Princess
Toadstool" to "Peach".

Comments are welcome.  Please enjoy.

         Yours truly - -
                        Princess Toadstool (Peach)
