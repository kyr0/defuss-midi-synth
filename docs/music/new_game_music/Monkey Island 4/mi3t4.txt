Description:
The grrreat music at the end of the game on the rollercoaster where
it keeps changing to different styles. I've got all four styles here,
about 15-25 seconds of each for 1:25 seconds of niceness.
just in case you can't work it out, the sections are as follows:

1) herman toothrot/hanging corpse scenery - first 25 seconds
2) pirate ship scenery - next 15-20 seconds
3) torture chamber scenery  - next 15-20 seconds
4) snow scenery - last 25-30 seconds

THIS MUSIC IS OHSOBEST HEARD ON A WAVETABLE SOUNDCARD.
