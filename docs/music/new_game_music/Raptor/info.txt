Thanks to Furutsun who provided this additional info about the 'Night Mission.mid' tune":

The tune is from chapters 2 and 3 of the game, but is not played in chapter 1, therefore anyone with the shareware version of the game would not know the track.
The track itself is only played during the dark night missions, of which there is one in chapter 2 and one in chapter 3. it replaces one level song from the songs that are played in chapter 1 and repeated in chapters 2 and 3, I'm not sure which level the song is from though, level 5 or 6 maybe of chapeters 2 or 3, although I would suggest the track name to be named to "Night Mission" or something like that, even better if someone knew what level its from in chapters 2 and 3.
