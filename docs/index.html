Sound Engine

The Sound Blaster 16 and the SoundFont engine play built in. The Sound Blaster PCI 128, Gravis UltraSound, Roland SC-55, SC-55mkII, MT-32 and the AWE cards play with your own files, which stay on your device.

Drop your files on the list or here Packs (.dspack), ECW wavesets (EAPCI2M, 4M or 8M.ECW) and Roland ROM files, all at once. The list names each device by its maker, model and year, newest first. AWE packs play on a local build of their card (awe32.wasm, awe64.wasm), which the local server provides; elsewhere drop it with them.
Bergium draws the music once it plays.

    Bring your MIDI.

    Files stay on your device. A MIDI, RMI or MUS file plays in the player above, on either engine; an SF2 file replaces the SoundFont, a WOPL file the FM bank, either also Brotli-compressed (.br).

    Download your synthesized MIDI.

    Everything you play on these keys or on a Web MIDI keyboard is recorded. Download renders the recording as a WAV file (48 kHz, stereo, 16-bit) with the engine and instruments selected now; a pause of more than two seconds with no note held shrinks to two seconds. Nothing recorded yet.

    Engines

    Sound Blaster 16 (OPL3)
    One emulated Yamaha YMF262 (Nuked OPL3), driven by libADLMIDI game banks. Not an analog board model.
    Sound Blaster 16 (CQM)
    One emulated Creative CQM, the OPL3-compatible chip of later Sound Blaster 16 cards (Nuked CQM), with the same game banks.
    SoundFont
    Sample-based TinySoundFont, not hardware emulation.
    Your hardware files
    The ROMs of the Sound Engine section run emulators of their chips with your own files, which stay on your device. Drop them all at once: packs (.dspack, which scripts/profile-assets.py makes), the ECW wavesets of the Sound Blaster PCI 128 (EAPCI2M.ECW, EAPCI4M.ECW or EAPCI8M.ECW, 2 to 8 MB: the wavetable sample bank of its Ensoniq AudioPCI drivers), and the Roland SC-55, SC-55mkII and MT-32 ROM files themselves, which the page identifies by their hashes. The list names each device by maker, model and year, newest first. An AWE pack plays on its card's local build, which only the local demo server provides. The page keeps the files you play in this browser's storage, so a later visit plays them without asking; Forget your files deletes them.

    On the audio clock

    Synthesis in an AudioWorklet: notes and file messages act at their output frame, and a song switches engines without losing its place. No account, upload or remote processing; the instrument runs here.
